Golden Wyrm Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This policy covers the clip uploader of Golden Wyrm Studio ("the app", "we"): an internal command-line tool that sends video clips of the studio's games to a TikTok account as drafts.

What the app receives from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok gives the app an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and the list of permissions granted. The app asks for one permission only, video.upload: it does not read your profile, followers, videos, messages or any other data.

Where it is kept

These tokens are stored only in a file on the computer that runs the app, readable by its user alone. They are sent to nobody but TikTok, over HTTPS, to upload drafts and to renew access. The app has no server, no analytics, no cookies and no advertising, and we never sell or share any data.

Videos

The app uploads only the video files its user chooses, made by the studio, to that user's own TikTok inbox. Captions are never sent by the app; they are written in the TikTok app.

How long

The tokens are kept until you disconnect: logging out of the app revokes its access at TikTok and deletes the file. Without use, TikTok ends the access after at most 180 days. You can also remove the app at any time from your TikTok account's settings (Security, then Manage app permissions).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
